A transparent open U-shaped tube holds a dense liquid in both arms and the connecting bend, with a lighter immiscible oil column above the dense liquid in the right arm. The two free surfaces start at different heights while the bottom valve is closed. An actuator opens the valve and withdraws. Both liquid levels move and settle with the two free surfaces at the same height, as if the lighter oil contributed the same hydrostatic pressure per unit depth as the dense liquid, although the liquids and their actual masses remain unchanged.
